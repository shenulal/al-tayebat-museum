# Al Tayebat International City — concept website

A concept website and ticket-booking flow for Al Tayebat International City for Science and Knowledge (the Abdul Raouf Khalil museum), Al Faisaliyah, Jeddah. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step.

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` — the site and the booking studio
- `brand/` — logo files and the generator that produced them (`brand/README.md`)

Run locally with any static server, for example `npx serve .`

Prices, time slots, add-ons and hours beyond Sunday to Thursday are illustrative and not an offer from the museum. Photographs are Creative Commons via Wikimedia Commons (credits on the page); videos are embedded from YouTube and remain the property of their creators. Concept by HelloPixels.
